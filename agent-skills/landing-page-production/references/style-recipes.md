# Landing Page Style Recipes

Choose one recipe per generation, then adapt it to the business and research.
Do not copy section names mechanically; use the recipe to shape composition,
palette, media, motion, and content rhythm.

## Selection Rule

- Pick a recipe that fits the audience and offer.
- State the recipe name in the plan or code comments only when useful.
- Do not reuse the same recipe for consecutive similar businesses when another
  direction would fit.
- Mix at most two adjacent recipes; avoid visual mush.

## Recipes

### Editorial Travel Magazine

Use for travel, hospitality, culture, restaurants, real estate, destinations.
Large photographic hero, strong serif/sans pairing, magazine-like pull quotes,
itinerary/proof blocks, destination cards with irregular rhythm, warm neutrals
plus one vivid accent. Motion should feel like page turns, slow reveals, or
map/route movement.

### Boutique Concierge

Use for premium local services, agencies, clinics, advisors, private offers.
Quiet luxury, strong negative space, high-contrast typography, small trust
details, timeline/process section, concierge-style CTA. Palette should combine
off-white/charcoal with one material accent such as brass, emerald, ruby, or
marine blue. Avoid generic glass cards.

### Cinematic Product Launch

Use for products, apps, AI tools, creator tools, events, launches. First viewport
should feel like a trailer: immersive media, bold claim, kinetic subcopy,
feature beats, social proof, demo/preview, FAQ. Motion can use staggered reveals,
spotlight sweeps, and progress/sequence cues.

### Local Authority Guide

Use for local companies, professional services, legal/accounting/maintenance,
public-record-heavy research. Lead with specificity, service area, history,
credentials, process, common questions, and practical next steps. Visual system
can use maps, stamps, document layers, city/neighborhood cues, and restrained
color contrasts.

### Playful Commerce Pop

Use for retail, food, beauty, creators, community campaigns. Bold color blocking,
large product/service moments, stickers/badges used sparingly, social sections,
offer bundles, urgency, and tactile UI. Motion can bounce or slide, but keep text
legible and controls stable.

### Technical Trust System

Use for B2B SaaS, infrastructure, security, developer tools, AI ops. Dense but
calm layout, diagrams, metrics, workflow strips, integration rows, comparison,
security/FAQ. Palette should not be one-note dark blue; add a real secondary
system color and crisp white/gray surfaces.

### Founder Letter

Use for consultancies, new ventures, personal brands, waitlists, high-touch
services. Editorial letter hero, founder promise, evidence timeline, principles,
service packages, objections, direct CTA. Make it feel authored, not templated.

### Destination Map Story

Use for travel, logistics, delivery, events, field services, regional brands.
Hero built around map/path/route metaphor, sequenced sections like stops,
coordinate labels, practical service details, and visual anchors for each step.
Motion can trace paths or reveal stops with reduced-motion fallback.

## Anti-Patterns

- Repeating equal cards for every section.
- Purple/blue gradient hero with floating blobs.
- Generic "benefits/features/testimonials" copy without business details.
- CTA-only pages with fewer than six meaningful sections.
- Images used as dark blurred backgrounds that hide the actual subject.
- JSON-LD or social claims not backed by evidence.
